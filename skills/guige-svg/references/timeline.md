# Timelines

Use a horizontal timeline for a small number of milestones and a vertical timeline for long descriptions or narrow canvases.

- Draw one clear axis with high-contrast event markers.
- Alternate cards above and below a horizontal axis to reduce collisions.
- Put the date or period nearest the marker, followed by title and short description.
- Use spans or shaded bands for durations; use points only for discrete events.
- Encode event categories with both color and a label or icon.

For more than eight events, group them into eras or phases, widen the canvas, or split the timeline. Do not compress labels until they become unreadable.
